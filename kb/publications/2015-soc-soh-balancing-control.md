title: Balancing control based on states of charge and states of health estimates at cell level

year: 2015

Cell-level active balancing driven by per-cell state-of-charge (SoC) and state-of-health (SoH) estimates — coupling state estimation with balancing control, an early link between the battery-management hardware at Freemens and the SoH-driven analytics later built at Altergo. From the Freemens / G2Elab (Grenoble INP / CNRS) collaboration; Taormina, Italy.
