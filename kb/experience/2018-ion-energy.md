## Context
ION Energy was founded in 2016 by **Akhil Aryan** — **incorporated in the United States and headquartered in Mumbai**, with operations across India — to improve the life and performance of lithium-ion batteries for electric vehicles and energy storage. In 2018 ION acquired **Freemens**, gaining close to a decade of battery-management engineering depth, a proven product line, and a base in France. Alexandre joined as co-founder and CTO.

## Role
Co-founder and CTO. Built and led an organisation that grew to roughly **70 engineers** spanning hardware, firmware, and software, operating across France and India. Owned the technology roadmap end to end, from BMS electronics to the cloud-analytics layer.

The organisation was structured as a set of specialised teams coordinated across two countries: three hardware teams, three embedded-systems teams, three test-and-validation teams, three application-engineering teams, and a dedicated systems and functional-safety (FuSa) team.

## What we built
- A battery technology platform covering BMS **hardware, firmware, and software**, built to accelerate customers' design, integration, and manufacturing and to support lasting field deployments.
- A chemistry-agnostic BMS hardware portfolio with 200+ configurable parameters, segmented by use case: **FS-LT** for electric two- and three-wheelers, **FS-CT** for micro-mobility, **FS-XT** for high-voltage applications (passenger cars, commercial vehicles, industrial, ESS), and an automotive-grade **TCU** telematics unit providing cloud connectivity.
- **UDYR & NEXUS** — a battery-swapping ecosystem for light electric mobility: **UDYR**, a portable 48 V lithium-ion battery for electric scooters (2018–2019), and **NEXUS**, a battery-swapping station. ION ultimately **licensed** the ecosystem to **Exicom** (India) rather than manufacturing it in-house.
- **Edison Analytics** — a battery intelligence and digital-twin platform, launched in 2019, that uses battery data, software analytics, and machine learning to remotely monitor, predict, and extend battery life. Renamed **Altergo** and ultimately spun out as a standalone company in 2021 alongside the Amazon-led pre-Series A.
- Transferred Freemens technology into ION and built up remote engineering teams to maintain, extend, and develop new battery-management products.
- **Internal engineering infrastructure** — a custom system-engineering and requirements-management platform, and in-house hardware-in-the-loop (HIL) platforms for BMS test and validation.

## Customers and partnerships
- **Hero MotoCorp (HMCL)** — ION became the sole BMS supplier to Hero MotoCorp, the world's largest two-wheeler manufacturer. The programme delivered **India's first two-wheeler BMS compliant with both ISO 26262 (functional safety) and AIS-156**, with **swappable and parallelisable** battery-management capabilities, and later scaled into volume production under Maxwell Energy Systems.
- **Ola Electric (2018)** — designed the battery-management system for Ola's electric two-wheelers, working directly with Ola's engineering leadership; the design did not go into production.
- Other Indian OEM relationships in the same period included **Tata Motors** and **Okinawa**, making ION one of the most-deployed BMS suppliers across India's two- and three-wheeler EV segment.
- **Exicom (India)** — licensed ION's **FreeSafe LT** (FS-LT) low-voltage BMS and the **UDYR** 48 V swappable lithium-ion battery (2018).
- **Multi-million BMS licensing** — signed multi-million-dollar BMS licensing and platform deals across **India, Europe, and the US**:  - **Wamtechnik (Poland)** — March 2020 strategic partnership; ION licensed its BMS and analytics platform for a 48 V / 456 Ah, 14S16P electric-excavator pack rated for operation down to –40 °C. The partnership carried a minimum revenue commitment of $1M in 2020 ramping toward $15M, and continued through the Maxwell phase.
  - **IBS (France)** — October 2019 partnership making ION the sole BMS supplier for IBS battery packs, with IBS planning to extend manufacturing into India in 2020/21.

## Scale and traction
- A cross-border France / India engineering organisation that grew to around 70 people, with a stated plan at the time of the 2021 raise to push past 125.
- Deployment trajectory: ~15,000 vehicles in early 2020, ~30,000 by mid-2020, and **65,000+ smart BMS units** by the time the hardware business moved into Maxwell in 2022. Customers spanned roughly 12–15 countries; 100% of BMS manufacturing was localised in India.
- **~700 MWh** of battery capacity under management on the Edison/Altergo analytics platform at the time of the 2021 round.
- **Funding:** a **$3.6M pre-Series A** round announced 1 July 2021, led by Amazon's **Climate Pledge Fund** — Amazon's first investment from that fund outside the United States — alongside **Climate Capital**, **YourNest Venture Capital**, **Riso Capital**, and **Venture Catalysts**. Proceeds were earmarked for engineering growth and expansion of the SaaS layer in North America and Europe.

## Outcome
ION's advanced-electronics and BMS hardware business was organised as **Maxwell Energy Systems** and acquired by **Endurance Technologies** in a deal announced 18 May 2022 and closed 1 July 2022 — $40M in total, of which Endurance took 51% up front for ~$17.5M and the balance in phased tranches over five years. The **Altergo** software business was retained as a separate company. Alexandre moved on to lead technology at Maxwell through the acquisition.
