## Context
Maxwell Energy Systems carried forward ION Energy's advanced-electronics and battery-management hardware business, headquartered in **Bengaluru** with a dedicated SMT manufacturing line in **Aurangabad** inside Endurance's industrial footprint. In May 2022, **Endurance Technologies** — a large Indian Tier-1 automotive component supplier — acquired Maxwell for **$40M** (51% acquired up front for ~$17.5M, the balance phased over five years). Maxwell's BMS became Endurance's entry point into EV-specific products, with founders Akhil Aryan and Alexandre Collet committing to lead the integration through the earn-out window.

## Role
Co-founder and CTO through the acquisition and the integration of a startup-built technology business into a Tier-1 industrial group. Responsible for the hardware, firmware, and software technology behind Maxwell's battery-management systems, and for raising that technology to automotive-grade, volume-manufacturing standards inside an OEM-facing supplier. Scaled the engineering organisation to roughly **100 engineers**.

The product portfolio under that organisation spanned low-voltage BMS (FS-LT, CT-Safe, CT-Lite family), high-voltage BMS (FS-XT), high-power BMS below 100 V (HP-Safe), smart power-distribution units based on solid-state relays (SB-B / SB-PR-I), an automotive-grade telematics unit (TCU), on-board chargers, and motor control units — with active technology directions in battery power networking and meshing, multi-chemistry support (sodium-ion and solid-state), functional safety (FuSa), cloud-based telemetry and ML remote diagnostics, OTA and cybersecurity, software-defined architecture, and in-house HIL and product-lifecycle-management (PLM) digital solutions.

## Customers and programmes
- **Royal Enfield** — developed a custom BMS for Royal Enfield's first electric motorcycle, the **Flying Flea C6**, based on Maxwell's **HP-Safe** platform (high-power, functionally safe, below 100 V), working closely with Royal Enfield's R&D team and CTO.
- **Hero MotoCorp (HMCL)** — scaled into volume production the BMS programme originated at ION, which delivered India's first functionally safe two-wheeler BMS. Worked directly with HMCL's Tech Center in Germany on the start-of-production (SOP) BMS and on high-performance solid-state-relay prototypes.- **Wamtechnik (Poland)** — continued the battery-management technology partnership with the Polish battery-pack manufacturer, begun during the ION Energy phase.
- **Replus (India)** — supplied battery-management systems to the Indian battery / energy-storage company.

By the end of this period Maxwell had **sold functionally-safe BMS in the millions of units**, with **85+ customers** and reach across more than **15 countries**, and an order book in the hundreds of crores of rupees at the time of the Endurance acquisition.

## Focus areas
- Maturing BMS hardware and firmware toward automotive qualification and functional-safety requirements.
- Supporting the transition from venture-stage product development to volume manufacturing within Endurance's industrial footprint.
- Continuity of the engineering organisation and technology roadmap through a change of ownership.

## Notable collaborators
- **Umesh Krishnappa** — CTO (EV), Royal Enfield (previously CTO at Ola Electric). Development of Royal Enfield's first electric motorcycle.
- **Georg Dobler** — HMCL Tech Center Germany. Start-of-production BMS and high-performance solid-state-relay prototypes.

## Why this chapter matters
Moving battery technology from a venture-funded startup into a Tier-1 automotive supplier is a different discipline from inventing it. It demands automotive qualification, functional safety, volume manufacturing, and direct OEM customer relationships — operating at industrial scale, not only at startup scale.

## Outcome
Alexandre remained CTO of Maxwell until March 2025, aligned with Endurance's move to a **100% buyout of Maxwell** (announced 8 May 2025) that unwound the founder earn-out structure. He then moved full-time to **Altergo**.
