## Context
After graduating from Grenoble INP — ENSE3 in 2009, Alexandre joined **ACCIUM**, an incubation project at **G2Elab** (the Grenoble Electrical Engineering Laboratory, Grenoble INP / CNRS) set up to turn power-electronics research into a company. He worked alongside researcher **Jean-Christophe Crébier**, a specialist in power-electronics silicon integration. From 2010 to 2011 he held the formal post of **CNRS research engineer** deployed to the project, with the explicit objective of spinning out a venture. ACCIUM was the working name the project carried before it was incorporated as **Freemens** in October 2011.

## What I did
- Designed and built a **next-to-next active balancer (5 A)**, coupling an inverter topology with an isolated converter.
- Designed and built a **high-power, multi-cell battery emulator** (hardware and software) dedicated to **active-BMS test and validation** in a hardware-in-the-loop (HIL) environment.

## Why it matters
This research was the technical and entrepreneurial seed for everything that followed: ACCIUM became **Freemens**, co-founded with Jean-Christophe Crébier in 2011, and the emulation and balancing work fed directly into the products built there.

## Publications
- *Multi-cell battery emulator for advanced battery management system benchmarking.* Collet, A., Crébier, J.-C., Chureau, A. (2011). Proceedings — ISIE 2011, IEEE International Symposium on Industrial Electronics.
- *An optimized topology for next-to-next balancing of series-connected lithium-ion cells.* Phung, T.H., Crébier, J.-C., Chureau, A., Collet, A., Van Nguyen, T. (2011). IEEE Transactions on Power Electronics.
