## Context
Altergo is a battery-intelligence cloud platform ([altergo.io](https://altergo.io)). It originated as **Edison Analytics** inside ION Energy, was renamed Altergo in 2019, and was established as its own company in 2021. When ION's hardware business was sold to Endurance Technologies, Altergo was retained as the standalone software business. Alexandre co-founded Altergo and worked on it in parallel with his Maxwell CTO role from **2022**, then moved to it **full-time in March 2025**.

## Role
Co-founder and CTO, leading a focused team — deliberately far smaller than the hardware-heavy organisations at ION Energy and Maxwell, reflecting Altergo's pure-software model. Co-founded with Akhil Aryan (CEO).

## What Altergo does
Altergo applies data science and machine learning to battery data so that owners and operators of battery fleets can monitor, predict, and extend the life of their assets after deployment:

- **Digital-twin modelling** — an ontological representation of cells, packs, sites, and sensors, with dynamic simulation underneath.
- **Battery intelligence IP** — a library of **44 proprietary models and functions** covering state of health (SoH), state of function (SoF), degradation, thermal, and failure-risk modelling, validated across chemistries.
- **Applications layer** — BESS sizing and solution design, predictive maintenance, augmentation planning, warranty management, revenue optimisation, and fleet dashboards.
- **Integrations** — SDK and APIs for EMS and SCADA systems, so the analytics layer plugs into existing operational stacks.

Roughly **700 MWh** of battery capacity is under management on the platform, with publicly cited outcomes including ~15% asset-life extension, recovery of 1.5%+ usable capacity, and prevention of a majority of early-life BESS failures.

## Customers
- **AWS** — battery intelligence for data-centre UPS energy storage.
- **Volvo** — battery analytics for electric trucks.
- **Plus Power** (US) — energy-arbitrage simulation and storage optimisation.
- **Beta Technologies** — battery intelligence for autonomous / electric flight.
- **esVolta** — supplied the platform across esVolta's portfolio of utility-scale battery storage projects in North America, originally committed at **581 MWh** (the pilot 8 MWh site in Irvine simulated savings of ~$450k/year).
- **Adani** — consulting from 2025 to deploy Altergo's battery intelligence on Adani's **30 GWh** energy-storage project in **Khavda, India**.
- Additional deployments visible in industry trackers include **Wamtechnik** and **Zenobē**.

## Why this chapter matters
Altergo completes a **17-year arc from silicon to cloud**. Alexandre started in power electronics and ASIC design, built hardware-led companies in Freemens and ION, and is now building the pure-software, data-driven layer of the same domain — the clearest demonstration of breadth across the full battery stack.
