Article by Alexandre on the Matrice blog. Its argument: a chat advises, an agent acts, so agents take over the method and the team's job becomes the intent: what is worth doing, and whether it is any good. It covers owning the why, what the new job actually takes, who takes to it and who struggles, the ways it goes wrong, and where to start. It presents this as a harder shift than it looks.
