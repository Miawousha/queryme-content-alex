Article by Alexandre on the Matrice blog. Its argument: most teams that ask for help with AI don't have an AI problem, they have a tool problem. It sets out the pattern Matrice has built five times (including the Truva mapper, Eloom, Truva Academy, Truva Intelligence and Fixit), in four phases.

- **Before you build:** pick the real bottleneck and write the record the work produces.
- **Build the tool and get the experts on it:** build the domain app and its CLI as one, and get the experts to switch to it.
- **Open it to agents:** teach agents the domain through the CLI and a skill, and measure the agent with evals before anyone relies on it.
- **Hand the work over and close the loop:** delegate a little, then a lot, and feed results back into the tool.
