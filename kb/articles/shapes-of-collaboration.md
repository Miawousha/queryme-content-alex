Article by Alexandre on the Matrice blog: a framework for understanding how collaborating systems work, and what changes when the collaborators are LLM agents. Part 1 sets out the framework (four components, three dimensions and the main models of collaboration). Part 2 applies it to agents: stigmergy for agents that never meet, redundancy over optimization when work is cheap, memory designed to be perishable, decaying the references rather than the content, memory that knows who is asking, and two open questions.
