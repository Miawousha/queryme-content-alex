Article by Alexandre on the Matrice blog, the first of a planned series on stages of delegation. It describes six stages of working with AI: alone, with a chat, with one agent, with several agents, through one agent that runs the others, and through a tree of agents. At each stage it looks at what the work becomes for the person: from doing it, to asking for it, to deciding what is wanted and whether you got it. It frames every stage with the same three pieces (you, the AI and the work), walks through a working day at each stage, and closes with what stays with the person and two caveats.
