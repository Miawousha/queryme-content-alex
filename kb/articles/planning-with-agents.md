Article by Alexandre on the Matrice blog, a reply to a published essay on planning with agents. Its position: agents can now plan and execute, so the human job is to agree on the goal, decide upfront how the result will be judged, and watch whether the work is converging. It develops three tools for that (a shared picture of the goal, a convergence view, and acceptance criteria set before the work starts) and draws on what military doctrine says about giving intent rather than orders.
